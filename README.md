# Tether USD (USDT) ERC-20 on Ethereum Network

Smart contract for Tether USD (USDT) to support seamless over-the-counter (OTC) digital asset trading, liquidity management, and cross-border settlement within the Offchain Market ecosystem.

## Contract Information

- **Token Name:** Tether USD
- **Symbol:** USDT
- **Decimals:** 6
- **Total Supply:** 200,000,000,000 USDT
- **Network:** ETHEREUM (ERC-20)
- **Contract Address:** `0x1c762b87c4699b0C8fE029147DD7937a9A1b0E08`

## Features

- **Standard ERC-20 implementation** with minting and burning capabilities.
- **1:1 Parity system** integrated with Offchain Market liquidity pools.
- **Simulators and Web3 integration** for secure and transparent OTC transactions.

## Verification & Deployment

The contract is verified on BscScan. Deployments are secured by the OpenZeppelin and Ownable standards.
